## Teaching

<p><strong>Design and Theory of Algorithms</strong> (CSE 431/830) &mdash; Michigan State University, Fall 2026.</p>
<p>Co-teaching with <a href="https://cse.msu.edu/~dolsonem/">Emily Dolson</a> and <a href="https://www.cse.msu.edu/~kjl/">Kevin Liu</a>. Analysis of algorithms and algorithm design techniques; efficient algorithms for classical problems; intractable problems and the techniques used to handle them.</p>
<p><a href="https://emilydolson.github.io/CSE-431-FS-2020/">Course page &rarr;</a></p>

## Coding Summer Camp

<p><strong>Making a Game of It</strong> &mdash; a week-long residential summer camp at Michigan State University where rising juniors and seniors design and build a video game in Python, as an introduction to software development and MSU's Computer Science program.</p>
<p>Co-instructor, 2023&ndash;2025; lead instructor starting 2026. Held annually each July, roughly 20 students per session.</p>
<p><a href="https://spartanyouth.msu.edu/resources/m/making-a-game-of-it-mgit-residential/">Program page &rarr;</a></p>
