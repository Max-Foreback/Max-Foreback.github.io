Since starting my PhD, I've been working with [Emily Dolson](https://cse.msu.edu/~dolsonem/), [Sydney Leither](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=BzcWiSgAAAAJ&hl=en), and the [Baum lab](https://baumlab.botany.wisc.edu/) to investigate pre-genetic adaptive change. While the Baum lab does the wet-lab side, I work computationally, modeling systems representative of the early interactions of pre-biotic chemicals.

A full summary of this framework is in *The ecology–evolution continuum and the origin of life*, Baum et al. 2023.
